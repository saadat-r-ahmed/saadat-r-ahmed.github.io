# CSE422 - Artificial Intelligence

## Course Overview

This course provides an introduction to artificial intelligence concepts, algorithms, and applications. Students learn fundamental AI techniques including search algorithms, knowledge representation, reasoning, and machine learning basics.

## Course Topics

- **Introduction to AI**: History, applications, and ethical considerations
- **Search Algorithms**: Uninformed search (BFS, DFS, UCS), Informed search (A\*, Greedy)
- **Game Playing**: Minimax algorithm, Alpha-beta pruning
- **Knowledge Representation**: Logic, semantic networks, frames
- **Reasoning**: Forward and backward chaining, resolution
- **Uncertainty**: Probability theory, Bayesian networks
- **Machine Learning**: Supervised learning, decision trees, neural networks
- **Natural Language Processing**: Basic text processing and understanding
- **Expert Systems**: Rule-based systems and knowledge engineering

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course, students will be able to:

- Understand fundamental concepts and applications of artificial intelligence
- Implement various search algorithms for problem-solving
- Design and develop knowledge-based systems
- Apply machine learning techniques to real-world problems
- Evaluate AI systems and understand their limitations
- Understand ethical implications of AI technologies
